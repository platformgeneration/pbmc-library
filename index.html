Platform Generation connects platform research with practical case analysis. The library helps researchers, students, strategists and practitioners understand how platforms coordinate consumers, providers, partners and owners around shared value creation.

What is the PBMC?

The Platform Business Model Canvas

The Platform Business Model Canvas (PBMC) maps platform business models and digital ecosystems. It makes the Consumer, Provider, Partner and Owner perspectives visible around a Core Value Unit and shows how governance, access, activities, resources and core transactions fit together.

What is a platform business model?

A platform business model creates value by orchestrating interactions between multiple participant groups rather than only producing and selling through a linear pipeline.

How do platform ecosystems work?

Platform ecosystems coordinate participants through shared rules, access mechanisms, incentives and transactions. The PBMC makes those relationships comparable across industries.

How do you analyse a platform?

Start with the Core Value Unit, identify the key actors, map their jobs, gains and pains, then trace the few transactions and governance mechanisms that make the ecosystem work.

Why compare platform cases?

Using one analytical framework across finance, commerce, mobility, public-sector and infrastructure ecosystems makes structural similarities and differences easier to see.
