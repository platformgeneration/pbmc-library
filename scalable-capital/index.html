Platform Lens Case 001

Scalable Capital

How AI agents can become a new access channel — and potentially reshape the platform partner architecture.

Platform Lens

AI agents move from being merely a technology layer to becoming an access channel between investors and the financial platform — changing how demand reaches the ecosystem.

Highlighted fields are especially affected by the AI-agent shift.
What changed?

The access architecture opens up

The central shift is not simply “more AI”. It is a change in who can mediate the relationship between customer and platform.

Before

  • Customer enters mainly through Scalable’s own app or web interface.
  • Scalable controls most of the interaction layer.
  • External technology supports the platform but does not normally represent the customer.
→

After

  • An AI agent may become an additional customer-facing access channel.
  • Part of discovery and interaction can happen outside Scalable’s own interface.
  • Partner and governance questions become more important: permissions, accountability and control.
Explore the model

Field by field

Access Channel

The most visible change. AI agents can sit between the investor and Scalable Capital, potentially becoming a new path through which demand reaches the platform.

Partners

AI platforms may evolve from ordinary technology suppliers into strategically important ecosystem partners if they mediate access, discovery or action on behalf of users.

Governance

Agent-mediated access raises questions about permissions, authentication, responsibility, execution authority and the boundary between advice and transaction.

Core Value Unit

The underlying value unit remains access to financial information, products and transactions. What changes is how users reach and interact with that value unit.
