Platform Lesson

Sharing the front door without giving up the regulated core

AI companies do not have to replace an existing platform to gain power. They can become a new Partner layer that owns the first conversation while the existing platform keeps the regulated transaction. Scalable's bet is that sharing the front door is better than forcing every customer back into its own app: the AI Partner can own the conversation while Scalable still owns the account, the rules and the execution.
