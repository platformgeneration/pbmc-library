Hey guys.

Welcome to this space for all platform enthusiasts, researchers, and pattern seekers out there.

We live in a world shaped by platforms. We are the Platform Generation: learning to read platforms, create them, and notice how they shape us in return.

I’m Davis, a guest researcher at the Technical University of Berlin, an economist and industrial engineer with a PhD in economics focused on platform business models. Over the years, I’ve worked across the public and private sectors on digital transformation, innovation, and new business models, and developed the Platform Business Model Canvas as part of my research.

I have analysed a wide range of platforms, and I see this site as my contribution to the ongoing conversation around platform business models, how they work, how they evolve, and how we can design them better.

Whether you want to understand an existing platform, compare different models, or structure your own idea, the PBMC helps make the key actors, value exchanges, governance mechanisms, and core transactions visible.
