Platform Lesson

National scale matters only when the marketplace becomes locally liquid

Owner Mode can make Akippa's supply side more scalable because parking Owners can onboard and manage capacity themselves instead of relying only on sales-led recruitment. But parking remains local and time-specific. The durable platform asset is therefore not the national member count by itself; it is local liquidity — enough Drivers and enough reservable spaces meeting around the same destination and time.

ConsumerPainFull / SearchThe pain is Full and Search: parking can be unavailable, and searching for a free space near the destination takes time.

ProviderResourcesSpace / TimeThe essential resources are Space and Time: a physical parking spot and the periods when it is available.

PartnerGainSmooth FlowTheir gain is smoother traffic flow and a more predictable arrival experience.
